- **GEORGE:** small brown monkey, tan face, big round eyes, curl of hair on top, no tail, very expressive.
- **MAN IN YELLOW:** tall slim man, all-yellow suit and tie, wide-brim yellow hat, round glasses, brown boots, carries a folded yellow umbrella.
- **BOY:** teenage boy, brown hair, freckles, navy baseball cap, red-and-white striped long-sleeve shirt, blue jeans, blue first-place ribbon pinned on his shirt.
- **BOY'S BOAT:** toy sailboat, green-and-white hull with portholes, white sails, small red-blue flag, controlled by a small black remote.
