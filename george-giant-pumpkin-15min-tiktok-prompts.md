# George and the Giant Pumpkin — TikTok Prompts (9:16)

## How to Use

- **New story:** completely separate from "George Builds a Boat". Same art style, new adventure.
- **Format:** Vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 24 fps, phone full screen.
- **Length:** 45 clips × 20 seconds = 15 minutes. Make one clip per prompt, then join them in order.
- **Copy:** Paste the whole yellow box for each clip. Every box has the style, characters and shots.
- **Safe zone:** Faces and action stay in the center. Top 10% and bottom 20% stay clear for TikTok buttons and captions.
- **No double:** Every box has a "No double" line. Keep it when you paste: it stops two Georges in one frame, repeated shots, and the previous clip playing again. When joining clips, put them end to end with no overlap.
- **Parts:** Act 1 (Clips 1–9) planting the seed, Act 2 (Clips 10–27) growing the pumpkin, Act 3 (Clips 28–45) the fair.

## Story

- **Act 1 · 0:00–3:00:** George sees a giant-pumpkin poster, gets one seed from Farmer Nell, learns a seed needs sun, water and soil, and meets his neighbor Lila, who is growing a pumpkin too.
- **Act 2 · 3:00–9:00:** George protects his vine from a hungry rabbit and a cheeky crow, learns how bees help flowers become pumpkins, builds a drip-bottle watering system in a heat wave, and saves the pumpkin from a storm with the yellow umbrella.
- **Act 3 · 9:00–15:00:** The pumpkin is too heavy to move, so George uses a lever and a ramp. After a runaway wagon ride, he helps Lila when her wagon breaks. Lila wins the blue ribbon, George wins a gold ribbon for care, and he saves the seeds for next spring.

## Character Sheet

- **GEORGE:** small brown monkey, tan face, big round eyes, curl of hair on top, no tail.
- **MAN IN YELLOW:** tall slim man, all-yellow suit and tie, wide-brim yellow hat, round glasses, brown boots.
- **FARMER NELL:** kind older woman, gray hair bun, straw hat, red checked shirt, blue overalls.
- **LILA:** girl about 8, curly black hair in two puffs, purple dress, green rain boots.
- **JUDGE:** bald older man with glasses, green shirt, suspenders, clipboard.
- **RABBIT:** small gray rabbit, fluffy white tail.
- **CROW:** cheeky shiny black crow.
- **BEES:** round fuzzy yellow-and-black cartoon bees.
- **THE PUMPKIN:** starts as a seed, then a sprout, a marble-sized green pumpkin, a striped green football-sized pumpkin, and finally a giant bright orange pumpkin almost as big as George.
