# George Builds a Boat — TikTok Prompts (9:16)

## How to Use

- **Format:** Vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920, phone full screen. Not 16:9 / 1280 × 720.
- **Length:** 13 clips × 20 seconds (about 4 min 13 s total). Make one clip per prompt, then join them in order.
- **Copy:** Paste the whole yellow box for each clip. Every box already contains the style, the characters and the shots.
- **Safe zone:** Faces and key action stay in the center. Top 10% and bottom 20% of the frame stay clear, because TikTok buttons and captions cover them.
- **No double:** Every box has a "No double" line. Keep it when you paste: it stops two Georges in one frame, repeated shots, and the previous clip playing again. When joining clips, put them end to end with no overlap.
- **Consistency:** Keep the same character descriptions in every clip so George, the boy and the man look the same throughout.

## Character Sheet

- **GEORGE:** small brown monkey, tan face, big round eyes, curl of hair on top, no tail, very expressive.
- **MAN IN YELLOW:** tall slim man, all-yellow suit and tie, wide-brim yellow hat, round glasses, brown boots, carries a folded yellow umbrella.
- **BOY:** teenage boy, brown hair, freckles, navy baseball cap, red-and-white striped long-sleeve shirt, blue jeans.
- **BOY'S BOAT:** toy sailboat, green-and-white hull with portholes, white sails, small red-blue flag.
- **GEORGE'S BOAT:** homemade raft of corks and wood pieces, a small bottle as mast, a spool and a pinwheel.
- **OLDER COUPLE:** woman in a straw hat and blue overalls; bald man with glasses, green shirt and suspenders.
