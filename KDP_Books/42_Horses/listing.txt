Galloping stallions, gentle mares, foals and dozens of horse breeds and riding scenes. Horses Extreme Dot-to-Dot for Adults is a challenging connect-the-dots puzzle book with 50 brand-new pictures, each one built from 1,000 to 2,000 numbered dots.

Inside you will discover pictures such as Arabian Horse, Hobby Horse Toy, Appaloosa with Spotted Blanket, Galloping Horse Weathervane, Horse Dozing in the Shade, Grazing Horse, Icelandic Horse in Tolt, Wild Mustangs Running, Riding Helmet, Boots and Crop, Chess Knight - and many more.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE THIS BOOK:
• 50 UNIQUE PUZZLES - every page reveals a different main picture
• 1,000 TO 2,000 DOTS per puzzle - a true extreme challenge for adults
• LARGE 8.5 x 11 INCH PAGES with clear, non-overlapping numbers
• HOLLOW START DOTS show exactly where each new line begins
• SINGLE-SIDED PAGES - no bleed-through, perfect for framing
• DIFFICULTY STARS and dot count printed on every page
• COMPLETE SOLUTIONS at the back of the book, 4 per page

Connecting thousands of dots is a relaxing, screen-free way to unwind, sharpen your focus and watch a beautiful picture appear line by line. A perfect gift for horse lovers and equestrians.
