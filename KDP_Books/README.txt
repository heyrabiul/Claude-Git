KDP BOOKS - 50 Extreme Dot-to-Dot for Adults niche books
============================================================

Each folder contains:
  *_interior.pdf   upload as the manuscript (8.5 x 11, no bleed, 117 pages, B&W)
  front_cover.jpg  front cover artwork (8.5 x 11 at 200 dpi)
  mockups/         10 marketing images (1600 x 1600)
  listing.txt      title, subtitle, description (text + HTML), 7 keywords, categories, price

All 50 interiors passed the automatic check (verify_book.py): 1,000-2,000 dots per puzzle,
no overlapping numbers or dots, KDP margins, blank backs, 4 solutions per page, 50 unique
main pictures per book and no picture repeated across books.
