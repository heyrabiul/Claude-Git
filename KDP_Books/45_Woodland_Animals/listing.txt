Badgers, beavers, wolves, bears, woodland birds and wildflowers from the forest. Woodland Animals Extreme Dot-to-Dot for Adults is a challenging connect-the-dots puzzle book with 50 brand-new pictures, each one built from 1,000 to 2,000 numbered dots.

Inside you will discover pictures such as Mole Popping Out of Its Molehill, Lynx with Tufted Ears, Pine Marten on a Bough, Woodcock in the Leaf Litter, White Trillium Flowers, Long-Nosed Shrew, Goshawk on a Stump, Ruffed Grouse Drumming on a Log, Striped Skunk, Warty Common Toad - and many more.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE THIS BOOK:
• 50 UNIQUE PUZZLES - every page reveals a different main picture
• 1,000 TO 2,000 DOTS per puzzle - a true extreme challenge for adults
• LARGE 8.5 x 11 INCH PAGES with clear, non-overlapping numbers
• HOLLOW START DOTS show exactly where each new line begins
• SINGLE-SIDED PAGES - no bleed-through, perfect for framing
• DIFFICULTY STARS and dot count printed on every page
• COMPLETE SOLUTIONS at the back of the book, 4 per page

Connecting thousands of dots is a relaxing, screen-free way to unwind, sharpen your focus and watch a beautiful picture appear line by line. A perfect gift for nature lovers and hikers.

Grab a pen, find dot number 1 and start connecting!
