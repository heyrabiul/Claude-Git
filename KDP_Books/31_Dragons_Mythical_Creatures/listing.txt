Fire-breathing dragons, griffins, phoenixes, hydras and legendary beasts from myth and fantasy. Dragons & Mythical Creatures Extreme Dot-to-Dot for Adults is a challenging connect-the-dots puzzle book with 50 brand-new pictures, each one built from 1,000 to 2,000 numbered dots.

Inside you will discover pictures such as Manticore with a Scorpion Tail, Mischievous Goblin, Kappa the River Imp, Dragon Perched on a Rock, Jackalope with Antlers, Griffin with Eagle Head and Lion Body, Dragon Rider in the Sky, Giant Roc Soaring over Mountains, Fire-Breathing Dragon, Cockatrice: Rooster-Headed Dragon - and many more.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE THIS BOOK:
• 50 UNIQUE PUZZLES - every page reveals a different main picture
• 1,000 TO 2,000 DOTS per puzzle - a true extreme challenge for adults
• LARGE 8.5 x 11 INCH PAGES with clear, non-overlapping numbers
• HOLLOW START DOTS show exactly where each new line begins
• SINGLE-SIDED PAGES - no bleed-through, perfect for framing
• DIFFICULTY STARS and dot count printed on every page
• COMPLETE SOLUTIONS at the back of the book, 4 per page

Connecting thousands of dots is a relaxing, screen-free way to unwind, sharpen your focus and watch a beautiful picture appear line by line. A perfect gift for fantasy fans and dragon lovers.

Grab a pen, find dot number 1 and start connecting!
